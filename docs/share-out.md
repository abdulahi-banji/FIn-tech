# 60-second share-out draft

Hi, we're Money Brain—our working team name. We're building a dashboard for
students who want to understand their money without checking several apps.

Our five features are account balances, categorized transactions, monthly spending
filters, large-purchase alerts, and answers to common money questions.

Today's prototype uses fictional data in Python and Streamlit. You can choose a
month, see where spending went, review a large purchase, and ask a question like,
“How much did I spend on dining?” Answers are calculated from sample transactions.
AI and bank connections come in later sessions.

Our next step is to confirm our team roles and review the design together. On
October 30, we'll connect Plaid Sandbox. By demo night, we want a clear, useful
view of a student's financial life.

Before presenting: confirm the name, introduce teammates and their roles, rehearse
to 60 seconds, and show the dashboard with October selected.
